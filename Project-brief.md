# Urban Growth Analysis of Adentan Municipality

## The Question

How has the extent of built-up land in Adentan Municipality changed between 2016 and 2025?

## Why It Matters

Urban growth affects land use, infrastructure, environmental sustainability, and planning decisions. Understanding where expansion occurs can help support better urban planning.

## Data Needed

1. Adentan Municipality Boundary
   Source: GADM- https://gadm.org-shapefile


2. Landsat 8 Imagery (2016)
   Source: https://earthexplorer.usgs.gov-GeoTIFF

3. Sentinel-2 Imagery (2025)
   Source: https://dataspace.copernicus.eu-JP2
4. Road and building data
    Source: https://www.openstreetmap.org-GeoPackage
## Method

- Download satellite imagery
- Load data into QGIS
- Clip imagery to Adentan Municipality
- Classify land cover
- Identify built-up areas
- Compare results between 2015 and 2025
- Calculate urban growth

## Expected Output

Maps, statistics, and visualizations showing urban expansion in Adentan Municipality between 2015 and 2025.
